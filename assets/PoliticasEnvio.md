At Line Commercial, our goal is to offer you the best shipping options, no matter where you are. We work daily with global distribution centers to ensure you receive your products as efficiently as possible.





###### **Order Processing and Shipping Time**

**Processing time**

Once payment is confirmed, you will receive an order confirmation email immediately after your purchase. You will then receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking number once your order has been processed. Orders take 1 to 2 business days to process.



Exception: During high-demand seasons (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas, New Year) processing times may be extended by 1 or 2 additional business days.



**Estimated delivery time**

Once your order has been processed and dispatched by our supplier, the estimated delivery time is 3 to 7 business days, depending on the destination and local postal service capacity.



Exception: Timelines may be affected by external factors beyond our control, such as customs holds, public holidays, extreme weather conditions, or high-demand seasons (such as Black Friday or Christmas).





###### **Order Tracking**

Once your package is handed over to the carrier, you will receive an email with your tracking number and a link to track your package in real time. You can track your package through our website or the global tracking platforms we will provide.



Tracking information may take 24 to 48 hours to update after the shipping confirmation is sent. If you do not receive a tracking number within 5 business days of your order confirmation, please contact us at Supports@linecommercial.com and we will investigate immediately.





###### **Split Shipments**

If your order contains multiple items managed by different suppliers, they may arrive in separate packages and at different times. You will receive individual tracking numbers for each shipment.



This is completely normal and does not indicate any issue with your order. No additional shipping charges will apply for split shipments.





###### **Shipping Rates and Costs**

We offer free standard shipping on all products.



###### **Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Packages**

Line Commercial is not responsible for packages that are lost or stolen after delivery confirmation by the carrier. If tracking shows your package was delivered but you have not received it, we recommend checking with neighbors, building management, or your local post office before contacting us.



If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 14 days of delivery at Supports@linecommercial.com, providing your order number and clear photographs of the damaged item and packaging. We will work with our supplier to issue a replacement or refund as appropriate.



For packages confirmed as lost by the carrier (not delivered), please notify us within the days following the estimated delivery date so we can file a claim with the carrier on your behalf.





###### **Incorrect Shipping Address**

It is the customer's responsibility to ensure the shipping address is entered correctly and completely. If you notice an error, please write to us immediately at Supports@linecommercial.com.



If the package has already been processed or shipped, we will not be able to modify the address. Line Commercial is not responsible for undelivered orders due to incorrect or incomplete addresses, or the recipient's absence at the time of delivery.

If you need to make a return or exchange (applicable only within the first 14 days after purchase for eligible items), please refer to our full Returns Policy section for details on product conditions.
